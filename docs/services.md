# Services

Matjar is a commerce platform, and the business behind it is a software
engineering team. We build Matjar for sellers who want a shop that is ready on
day one, and we build custom software for stores and businesses whose needs go
past what any product ships.

Both offers come from the same team and the same codebase. A custom build sits
next to your Matjar store instead of replacing it.

## What we build

| Service             | What it covers                                                                                                    |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Matjar commerce     | Storefront, seller dashboard, products, inventory, orders, payments, domains, and reporting.                      |
| Custom software     | Web applications, internal tools, and workflows that no off-the-shelf product covers.                             |
| Integrations        | Connections to ERP, accounting, payment, shipping, and warehouse systems so data stays in sync without re-typing. |
| Business portals    | Customer, partner, and staff portals that work from the same data as the store.                                   |
| Ongoing engineering | Support, maintenance, performance work, and new features after launch, with the engineers who built the system.   |

We take on work whether or not you run a Matjar store. The commerce platform is
the starting point, not the boundary of the business.

## Who builds it

Our engineers are professional software engineers with almost a decade of
experience delivering systems in esteemed organisations, including **ITworx**,
**Ejada**, **Bank Misr**, and **Suez Canal Bank**.

That background covers banking-grade delivery, enterprise integration, and
large-scale web applications. It is the same standard we apply to a two-person
shop and to a custom platform.

## How we start

1. **Tell us the problem.** Describe the workflow, the systems involved, and
   what is failing today.
2. **Map what already exists.** We check what Matjar covers out of the box
   before proposing custom work.
3. **Scope the build.** You get a written scope, the parts we would build, and
   the order we would ship them in.
4. **Build and launch.** We ship in small increments so you can use the work
   early and steer it.
5. **Stay on.** Support and further development are available after launch.

## Working with the platform

Custom work uses the same standards as the product: TypeScript and Next.js on
the web, shared design tokens, accessibility and RTL support, and automated
tests. There is no separate "agency stack" to maintain.
